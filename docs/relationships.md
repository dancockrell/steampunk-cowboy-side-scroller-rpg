# Adult goddess/nymph HaremLit framework

The long-term premise includes multiple mutually chosen relationships with explicitly adult women: goddesses and nymphs with goals, boundaries, rivalries and reasons to appoint Michael their earthly champion and lover. Romanceable characters have adult identities and adult visual presentation, including immortal characters. Michael is an adult man.

Relationships are reciprocal arcs, not collected rewards. Admiration can open a conversation; consent and an authored mutual choice establish intimacy. A blessing can follow a non-romantic alliance. Refusal, postponement and later reconciliation are valid states and cannot soft-lock progression.

## Slice patron (provisional)

**The Keeper of the Clay Dead** is a working role, with final name and appearance pending original-reference recovery. She is an adult goddess whose domain is remembrance, funerary vessels and the dignity of imperfect remains. Her agenda is to stop the temple's guardians from consuming the identities they protect. She values ingenuity and respect; needless destruction irritates her. She can admire Michael's audacity without agreeing with him.

The slice contains an introduction, a response to a witnessed choice, a champion/alliance offer and an optional mutually chosen romantic beat. Intimacy is conveyed through dialogue, portrait/manifestation acting and fade-to-black; explicit scene production is outside this setup. The single patron establishes the framework without requiring a second romanceable character.

## State and evidence

Relationship arc: unknown → encountered → tested → allied; a separate mutually chosen romance state is none/interested/courting/intimate/paused/ended. Alliance and romance are separate fields. Initial tracked dimensions are trust, respect and fascination; use small bounded tuning ranges later. Desire, irritation and ideological disagreement can be authored flags or dialogue conditions until a specific scene needs numerical tracking. Do not implement ten hidden meters simply because they appeared in brainstorming.

Witnessed facts such as `urn_preserved`, `supplicant_protected`, `burial_identity_restored` or `shrine_destroyed` carry stable event IDs and context. A heroine responds only to facts she witnessed or was told through a defined narrative event. Repeating a farmable action gives no unlimited relationship gain. UI communicates judgments in words and performances rather than exposing a romance optimization spreadsheet.

Each offer names its conditions and allows accept, defer or decline. A previous yes is not universal future consent. In the eventual multi-partner story, women know about relevant relationships; compatibility, rivalry and agreements are authored, and neither friendship nor intimacy follows from a single global harem score.

## Mechanical aid

One slice intervention: **Recall to Clay**, a provisional ability that returns one eligible weakened dead creature to its vessel, briefly clearing a route. Eligibility, cooldown, active-use limit and duration are explicit tuning fields. It cannot resolve every boss or replace the four tools. Michael gets a visible preview and clear unavailable feedback. Only one major manifestation occupies the action field at a time; portraits may carry dialogue outside combat.

If the player declines alliance, the temple remains completable using a slower physical tool route. Relationship flags and intervention access save with checkpoint progress; a reload cannot re-award the offer or erase a recorded refusal.
