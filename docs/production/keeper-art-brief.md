# Keeper of the Clay Dead: portrait and manifestation brief

Working role only; final name, face, costume, palette and iconography remain unapproved. She is explicitly an adult goddess with an adult appearance. Her authority comes from her relationship to memory and funerary vessels, not from youth coding or a generic reward pose.

## Performance direction

She studies Michael before offering approval. Warmth is deliberate, displeasure is controlled, and amusement suggests she has watched many mortals make similar mistakes. Sensuality can appear in posture, gaze and restrained intimacy while preserving her agency and domain. She should feel capable of saying no and remaining compelling.

## First deliverables

One neutral three-quarter portrait key, one full-body manifestation silhouette, and a shared identity note. The portrait can be painted/high-detail raster work; the world manifestation must remain clear beside chunky sprites. Keep face structure, hair silhouette and major costume landmarks consistent across scales. Do not compensate for a mismatched face with heavier glow.

Provisional portrait master: 1024×1536 RGBA, upper-body/three-quarter crop, with head and hands inside a safe inset. Export crop variants after UI composition is tested. Manifestation source uses a generous transparent canvas; final world dimensions wait for F02 and recovered references. These are proposed working canvases, not claims about the approved render.

## Expression set

| Expression | Direction | Narrative use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutral | Assessing gaze, composed mouth, relaxed authority | First appraisal and exposition |
| Amused | Small asymmetrical smile; eyes engage before mouth | Clever lasso solution or audacious reply |
| Displeased | Focused gaze, reduced warmth; no caricature sneer | Needless damage to funerary objects |
| Earnest | Softer guarded attention, stable adult identity | Champion offer and personal trust |

Keep camera, crop, face proportions, lighting direction and costume fixed across expressions. Do not regenerate a new character for each mood. Add eyelid/mouth motion only if a delivered scene needs it; four stable stills are more useful than unstable pseudo-animation.

## Manifestation sequence

Arrival: local dust/paint/torch reaction → silhouette coheres → full readable presence. Idle: minimal breath/fabric motion, no distracting perpetual effects. Intervention: attention shifts to an eligible dead creature → a single readable hand gesture → the creature returns to its vessel → effects settle. Departure reverses the material logic without implying she died.

The intervention must preserve view of Michael, enemy intent and landing spaces. Use one focal effect at a time. Dialogue portrait and action manifestation are coordinated presentations of the same character; their simultaneous appearance must not suggest two different goddesses.

## Acceptance and handoff

Deliver reference IDs, identity notes, expression filenames, crop/pivot metadata, layer breakdown, source files and native-context previews. The runtime pack is admitted only after face/costume continuity and full-window/world-scale composition are reviewed. No dialogue or relationship state is inferred from an expression filename; code selects presentation from the authored scene state.
