# Gallery of vessels: first room art and play staging

One calibration room, not another level added to the slice. This is the first gallery beat from the existing route. Use it to test Michael, lasso, jar emergence and ornate temple readability together. No final screen render has been produced.

## Composition proposal

At the provisional 640×360 world view, reserve a clear traversable floor band across the lower portion, a foreground pillar on the edge rather than over a landing, and a rear architectural opening with a slow parallax layer. A warm torch pool frames the first jar; the wall immediately behind Michael uses broader, quieter value shapes. Deep shadows remain visible without swallowing interactable outlines.

The first encounter occurs inside a comfortable camera frame. Michael sees the jar's tell from a safe standing area before stepping into its attack range. A lasso anchor and a simple counterweight occupy different silhouettes from ordinary decoration. A protected funerary vessel sits outside the shotgun plug's direct line so preservation is a readable choice, not hidden punishment.

## Three required camera-matched review plates

Before: Michael at rest, jar intact, route and anchor readable. Peak: jar halfway unfolded, Michael in lasso anticipation, no overlap hiding the emerging body. After: sentinel active or resolved, jar location still recognizable, required route remains visible. Keep camera, crop and lighting baseline identical so reviewers can judge the transformation rather than a changed composition.

These are future production plates, not permission to fabricate missing approved screenshots. Recover visual targets first, then use the same room plate to review all candidate sprites. A beauty shot at a flattering zoom cannot replace native-scale review.
