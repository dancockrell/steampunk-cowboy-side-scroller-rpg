# Implementation roadmap

## Foundation — this delivery

Design authority, art pipeline, implementation contracts, Godot shell, folders and backlog. Completion means coherent documentation and a clean published repository. It does not mean playable movement or finished art.

## M1: Visual and technical calibration

Recover originals, lock Michael identity/scale, pin engine, demonstrate world/portrait composition, build movement graybox and lasso spike. Codex concentrates on art and plans; Claude/Grok can implement controller and compositor tasks. Exit: reference register is truthful, native movement can be reviewed and an initial lasso loop works without clipping.

## M2: One complete room

Deliver first admitted Michael action pack, a room art kit, ceramic emergence, core weapon verbs and a combined puzzle. Exit: a small room proves simulation/presentation separation and looks recognizably consistent with the approved art at native scale.

## M3: Temple route and divine relationship

Add remaining two enemy families, the route, checkpoint behavior, Keeper dialogue choices, portrait/manifestation pack and one intervention with non-romantic progression. Exit: full route is playable, persistable and narratively coherent.

## M4: Polish and implementation handoff closure

Visual/motion inspection, sound/feedback pass, accessibility, soft-lock audit, performance measurement and documented clean-checkout build. Exit criteria are in vertical-slice.md. Further content planning waits until the slice demonstrates the production method.

No calendar or automatic future execution is promised. Use short bounded tasks and measured dependencies. Foundation is ready for implementation even while F01 remains an explicit art-admission prerequisite.
